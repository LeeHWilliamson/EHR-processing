Does this application use real patient data?

No. The tutorial uses a fixed cohort of synthetic patients generated with Synthea. These records do not represent real people.

How is the correct diagnosis established?

Ground truth comes from Synthea’s exact type 2 diabetes diagnosis in the complete synthetic record. It is not inferred by the clinical evidence rules.
