Is your AI intelligent? Is it compliant? Is it secure?

Getting satisfactory answers to all three of these questions simultaneously presents a new challenge for healthcare providers. Artificial intelligence can affect medical outcomes, information compliance, and data security at the same time. Evaluating an AI workflow may therefore require experts from very different backgrounds to work together and agree on what should be tested, how it should be tested, and what success actually looks like. These concerns are often evaluated separately, making it difficult to understand how they interact within a single AI workflow. Synth-EHR attempts to address that problem by giving users of almost any background a common environment for designing and running AI workflows on realistic synthetic patient records. The goal is to generate data that answers three questions at once: Was the AI medically accurate? Did it follow the expected workflow? And did it access and use patient information appropriately?

About this demo

The full Synth-EHR platform is still under development, but this webapp includes an interactive example of a Synth-EHR evaluation: identifying patients who likely have Type II diabetes.

Reaching the correct answer, however, is only part of the challenge.

By following the tutorial, you can explore a scenario where medical accuracy, workflow adherence, and information access all matter simultaneously. Whether the AI succeeds depends on much more than which model is performing the task. It also depends on how the AI agent is allowed to access patient data, the instructions it receives, and what the user considers to be an appropriate sequence of steps for reaching its conclusion.

The tutorial can be found using the button in the top-right corner of the window. The FAQs contain additional information about patient generation, AI usage, evaluation metrics, and some of the other design decisions behind Synth-EHR.
